Vassos Achilleos is a CNRS researcher (*Chargé de recherche*) at the Laboratoire d'Acoustique de l'Université du Mans (LAUM, UMR 6613), CNRS / Le Mans Université. His research covers acoustic waveguides and networks, nonlinear acoustics and sound absorption, and non-Hermitian and active wave physics. He is the principal investigator of the ERC Starting Grant NASA (*Nonlinear and Active Sound Absorption*, 2023–2028).
