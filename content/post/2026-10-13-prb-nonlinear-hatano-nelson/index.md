In a nonreciprocal Hatano–Nelson lattice with on-site cubic nonlinearity, non-Hermitian amplification makes the nonlinear frequency shifts grow in time, so that even a weak nonlinearity drives a wave packet through three successive regimes: coherent nonlinear-skin propagation, resonant wave mixing that destroys the packet, and self-trapping of a fraction of the norm while the remainder propagates ballistically. The crossover times are set by the width and mean level spacing of the eigenfrequency spectrum; in the first regime a variational Gaussian theory shows that focusing (defocusing) nonlinearity reduces (enhances) the packet acceleration.

With B. Many Manda.
